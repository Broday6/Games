How to play

Day: tap an empty tile for the build ring — walls, harvesters and towers. Drag across tiles to paint a line of walls. Before you confirm, the dashed line shows the new route the shadows will take.

Walls shape the maze. If you seal every path, the shadows smash through the weakest wall (marked with a red ✕) — so leave a long, winding road instead, or reinforce the wall they will hit with stone.

Tap trees, boulders and crystals to gather wood, stone and crystal (limited taps per day). Harvesters next to them pay out every dawn.

Night: the wave comes ashore. Change speed with the 1×/2×/3× button. Tap a tower to upgrade it or change its target (first, last, strong, close). Tier 3 offers a choice of two paths.

Flyers ignore walls. Burrowers tunnel under them. Wisps are invisible unless a Lantern lights them. A boss rises every 5th night. Survive 20 nights to win; after each night pick a boon.

Desktop: click to build · drag for walls · wheel to zoom · right-drag to pan · Space start night / speed · 1 2 3 speed or pick a boon · Esc close / pause
