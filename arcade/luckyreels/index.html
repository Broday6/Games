How to play

You own a 5 × 4 slot machine. Every spin, the symbols you own land in random slots and pay coins. If you own fewer than 20 symbols, some slots stay empty; if you own more, only 20 random ones show up.

Symbols interact with their neighbours — the 8 slots around them, diagonals included. A Cat drinks adjacent Milk, a Miner digs Ore, Bees double Flowers, the Moon triples Owls… Watch the arrows during a spin to see who helped whom.

After each spin, pick 1 of 3 new symbols to add (or skip). Tap any symbol or item to read exactly what it does.

Every few spins the Landlord wants rent, and it keeps rising. Can't pay? The run is over. Pay, and you climb a floor and choose an item — a permanent bonus.

Removal tokens delete a symbol from your machine (open your symbols with the bag button). Reroll tokens refresh the symbol choices. You earn one of each with every rent paid.

Pay all 13 rents to beat the Landlord. Winning unlocks higher Stakes, new landlords and starting sets, and an endless mode.

Hold anywhere during a spin to skip ahead, or turn on Fast.

Keys: Space spin/confirm · 1–4 pick · S skip · R reroll · B symbols · F fast · Esc menu

All coins are pretend. There are no purchases or real-money features.
