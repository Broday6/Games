How to play

Touch anywhere and drag to move — the stick appears under your thumb. On desktop use WASD or the arrow keys. Weapons fire on their own.

Collect tide gems to level up and pick upgrades. Max a weapon and own its partner passive, then open a chest from a glowing elite to evolve it.

Bosses rise at 3:00, 6:00 and 9:00. Survive to 10:00 and dawn wins the run.

Keys: Esc/P pause · 1 2 3 pick a card · R reroll · Enter continue
