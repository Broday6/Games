# Driftwood — how to play

Driftwood is a first-person co-op survival roguelike for 1–4 players. You wash ashore on a procedurally generated island,
gather and craft by day, survive escalating nights, kill ten bosses, repair the ship and escape. Runs are short (an hour or two);
between runs you spend Shards in the Camp on permanent upgrades, classes and hats.

## First ten minutes

1. **Look and move.** Mouse to look, `W A S D` to walk, `Shift` to sprint, `Space` to jump, `Q` to dodge-roll (dodges are limited — the ◆ counter).
2. **Punch a tree** (left mouse) for wood and sticks. Walk over drops to pick them up. Punch a rock for stone.
3. **Craft** with `Tab`. Make a **Stone Axe** and **Stone Pickaxe** (much faster harvesting), then a **Workbench**.
4. **Place** the workbench: select it on the hotbar (`1–9`) and click where you look. Stand near it for more recipes.
5. **Before dusk** (the clock at the top) craft a **Campfire** and a **Torch**. Monsters never spawn near light, and total darkness hurts.
6. **Eat** (`F`) when the Food bar drops: berries from bushes, mushrooms, cooked meat from a campfire.
7. **Night one**: stay by the fire, fight what comes. Kills give XP and coins; every level offers a **boon** (pick of three, `Alt+1/2/3` or click).
8. **Chests** cost coins and give everyone nearby a boon; rarer chests, better boons and weapons.
9. **The Dealer's Table** by the wreck: gamble coins on slots, dice, the Wheel of Fates or blackjack for coins, boons and hats.
10. **Altars**: each biome has one. Craft its totem, summon the guardian, kill it for a gem. Three gems repair the ship.
11. **Set sail** by depositing the repairs at the wreck. A siege and the Leviathan follow. Survive and you escape.

## Combat

- Left mouse: attack. Three hits in a row chain into a combo with a heavier finisher.
- Hold right mouse: heavy attack (melee), draw (bow), cast (staff), block (shield).
- Weapons roll **affixes** (Swift, Brutal, Vampiric, Flaming…) and rarity. Bosses drop unique weapons and armour with special effects.
- Downed players bleed out unless a friend holds `E` next to them. Everyone dead = run over (you still keep Shards).

## Nights and bosses

Nights get harder; from night 2 a **night boss** hunts you each night (Bonecrusher, the Warden, the Matriarch, Frostmaw, the Lich,
the Titan), plus the three altar guardians and the Leviathan — ten in all. Night events (blood moon, fog, swarm, eclipse, bounty)
change the rules for that night.

## Multiplayer

One player clicks **Host a game** and shares the 5-letter room code; friends **Join a friend** with it. The host runs the world;
everyone shares the island, fires, loot and boss fights. If room codes fail on someone's network, use **Manual invite** in the lobby
and swap the two codes over any chat. The desktop build works the same way.

## Settings

Everything is rebindable (Controls & settings). Auto quality keeps the frame rate up by lowering the internal resolution; the
quality slider is the ceiling. Toon outlines can be turned off on very old GPUs.
