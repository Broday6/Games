DRIFTWOOD

A co-op survival roguelike. Wash ashore, gather, craft, survive the nights, slay the three guardians, repair the ship, escape.
You'll get a 5-letter room code to share.
-----
Friends can also join after you start.
Manual invite (if room codes don't work — swap codes over Discord)
Host: 1) click Make invite, send the text to your friend. 2) Paste their reply and click Accept reply. Repeat for each friend.
Friend: paste the host's invite, click Make reply, send the reply back to the host.
